# Licensing and provenance

dcbench stores patches and annotations derived from the upstream repositories
listed in `repos.yaml`. Each patch remains subject to its source repository's
license; inclusion here does not relicense upstream code. Repository URLs and
exact source revisions are recorded in `repos.yaml`.

The diffctx-authored annotations, manifests, and metadata are distributed under
the repository's Apache-2.0 license. See `README.md` and
`ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md` for collection and annotation provenance.
