# Licensing and provenance

This corpus contains evaluation metadata and reviewer labels for commits from
React Native, Gitpod, and Sentry. Upstream source remains subject to each
project's license; commit identities are recorded in `commits.tsv`.

The diffctx-authored labels, summaries, and reports are distributed under the
repository's Apache-2.0 license. Collection and review methodology are
documented in `README.md`.
